# Brand decision: The Cash Ledger

Status: accepted by the brand strategist; the design evaluator judges it on the built product.

## Decision

The Cash Ledger

## Alternatives rejected

- **Fintech Glass**: Too generic. It looks like every other SaaS product and ignores the offline, cash-based reality of the user's environment. It feels distant and corporate.
- **Bank Vault**: Too institutional. It implies security through exclusion, which is the exact problem the product is trying to solve. It feels cold and untrustworthy to a small trader.

## Risks

- The paper texture might look dated if not executed with high-quality typography and spacing.
- The 'ledger' metaphor might be too literal for some analysts who expect a modern dashboard.

## Reversal

Revisit this decision when: If user testing shows that analysts find the tactile metaphor confusing or slow, we will shift to a cleaner, more data-dense interface while keeping the color palette and voice.

Product: Trust Ledger.
